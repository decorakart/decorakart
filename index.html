    Our story

    Bihar se Khurja tak – ek simple brand journey.

    Decorakart ek chhota sa brand hai jo ceramics ko simple, premium aur confusion-free banane ke mission par hai. Aap Bihar, Delhi ya India ke kisi bhi sheher se ho – hum chahte hain ki aapko Khurja ki best pieces easy language aur clear pricing ke saath milein.

    Inventory Khurja me rakha gaya hai, jahan se har piece check karke, safely pack karke ship hota hai. Website par jo bhi pieces live hain, unka order aap directly WhatsApp par bhej sakte hain – jisse hum aapko real photos, combos aur gift suggestions bhi de paate hain.

    How it works
